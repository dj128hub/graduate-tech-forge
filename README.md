# Digitise Connect

Build me a website called ‘Digitize’ utilising some black, white and digital teal colours. It should be a platform for an online business that connects computer science graduates with small businesses, to help upskill the graduates, whilst providing tech services such as website creation, general tech support, inventories and more. There should be subscription options for maintenance of services as well as the business owners paying once off for the creation of websites and advertisements by the graduates which should be ‘technical associates’. There may also be a portfolio scheme for the associates to showcase their skills and have a reliability score for when their work is successful. Also make a system by which the associates submit the required work for checking before sending to the businesses. Make it possible for associates to advertise their unique skills on their profiles so that I (as the owner of digitize business) can match the associates with the customers as needed, also make the platform so that customers can request specific services.
